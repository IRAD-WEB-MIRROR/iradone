      24 New Ambulances Reach Regional Hospital and Rural Health Posts

      Minister Kubatova hands over the vehicles in Talas, saying district crews will get the first call on winter runs.

      Published: November 16, 2026 · OneNews

      Photo: IradOne

      TALAS — The Ministry of Health handed over 24 new ambulances in Talas today, nine to the Talas Regional Hospital Medical Complex and fifteen to district hospitals and rural health posts in Kara-Buura, Lower Naryn, Toktogul and Sary-Bulak. Health Minister Dr Jamila Kubatova received the keys on behalf of the hospitals at a short ceremony in the hospital yard.

      The vehicles arrive as the heating season and the first hard frosts test the districts' emergency service. Talas recorded a low of -4°C today, and Irad Gidromet expects colder nights in the valleys. The Fergana Gateway on State Route 1 is open, but only to vehicles on chains or winter tyres, and the Kara-Buura Pass is closed for the winter.

      "Most of the vehicles we are replacing have been on the road since before the 2016 district reform, and in winter the oldest ones were the first to stay in the yard," Ms Kubatova said. "The new crews in the districts will answer calls from their own posts and will not wait for a car to come out from the city." She said every vehicle has a cab heater, a fixed stretcher and a set of chains in the locker.

      Dr Yosep Temirkulov, chief physician of the Regional Hospital and Blood Services Center, said the nine vehicles given to the hospital will reinforce the night shift and the transfer runs from the districts. "A patient from Tash-Kömür or Sary-Bulak now meets a working ambulance halfway instead of an empty road," he said. "The hospital will keep two cars in reserve through December and January, when calls are heaviest."

      Calls to the 103 line are free from any phone, mobiles included. Dr Temirkulov asked residents in the districts to give the dispatcher the village name and the nearest landmark, because many roads there have no street names.

      The ministry said the vehicles will be at their stations from Wednesday after registration with the Highway Patrol Corps. It will report on winter response times for the districts in the new year.
