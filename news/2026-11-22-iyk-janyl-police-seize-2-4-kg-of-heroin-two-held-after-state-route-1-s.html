      Iyık Janyl Police Seize 2.4 kg of Heroin; Two Held After State Route 1 Stop

      City police and the ministry's Lower Naryn detachment found the drugs hidden in a door panel and a spare-wheel well.

      Published: November 22, 2026 · OneNews

      Photo: Jonbart / Wikimedia Commons, Public domain

      IYIK JANYL — Iyık Janyl City Police, working with the Ministry of Internal Security's Lower Naryn detachment, have seized 2.4 kg of heroin from a car stopped on State Route 1 on the city's east bank on the evening of Thursday 19 November.

      Officers found the drugs in a door panel and in the spare-wheel well. Two men were detained at the scene: a 41-year-old resident of Bazar-Korgon (K.M., born 1985) and a 33-year-old resident of the city's Jogorku quarter (T.S., born 1993).

      Maj. Shavkat Yuldashev, press officer of Iyık Janyl City Police, announced the detentions on Friday. The two men are held under the Criminal Code article on illegal acquisition and carrying of narcotics on a large scale, pending a court decision on custody.

      "The consignment came down the Fergana rim route and was headed for the Lower Town and Tash-Kömür," Maj. Yuldashev said. Police name no criminal group, and the investigation continues.

      The seizure is modest beside the synthetic-opioid trade that police deal with in Nurzhan. It is large for a southern city where heroin is now mainly an older users' drug. The city's streets go into winter with that much less of it on them.
