      Five-Year Roads Record: 340 km Laid, 112 Machines Added, 38 Ploughs for Passes

      The Ministry of Transport and the Department of Infrastructure and Communications put 4.1 billion som into paving and machinery in 2022–2026, with the Trans-Talas Highway taking the biggest share.

      Published: November 24, 2026 · OneNews

      Photo: Irad Wiki Commons

      TALAS — The Ministry of Transport and the Department of Infrastructure and Communications published a five-year roads record for 2022–2026 this month, closing the books on the building season. It counts 340 km of the Special Economic Zone's roughly 4,800 km of public roads newly paved or resurfaced, and 112 units of road machinery bought or replaced.

      The machinery list covers pavers, rollers and graders, and 38 snowploughs for the pass depots. Spending on paving and machinery over the five years came to 4.1 billion som.

      Most of the length lies on two arteries: the Trans-Talas Highway between Talas, Kyzyl-Adyr and Talas North Terminal, and the lowland Talas–Nurzhan road, with a number of city avenues in Nurzhan added to the tally. Nurzhandorstroy, the Nurzhan road builder led by General Director Oleg Mironenko, won the larger share of the contract value.

      Transport Minister Oleg Rudenko said the programme had kept the main freight corridors moving. "A road is judged in January, when the snow comes, not at the ribbon-cutting," he said. The ministry said the new winter equipment goes first to the Fergana Gateway and Toguz-Bulak depots, which keep State Route 1 and the Kök-Bel road open.

      Department Director Arstan Medetov was careful about what the figures show. "This is a record of kilometres laid, not of roads finished," he said. "Some stretches still wait for their shoulders, drainage and markings, and we will say so each year."

      The numbers arrive as the 2027 budget goes to the Mejlis, where the road fund competes with wages, security and schools. The Talas Herald has said it will weigh the five-year figures against the road-fund share in that draft. For drivers on the Trans-Talas Highway, the ministry's message is that the surface is already in place.
