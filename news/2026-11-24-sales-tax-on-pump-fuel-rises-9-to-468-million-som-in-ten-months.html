      Sales Tax on Pump Fuel Rises 9% to 468 Million Som in Ten Months

      The State Tax Service credits more litres declared and tighter station audits, since pump prices did not move between January and October.

      Published: November 24, 2026 · OneNews

      Photo: Irad Wiki Commons

      TALAS — The State Tax Service collected 468 million som in sales tax on retail fuel in January–October 2026, up 9% on the 429 million recorded in the same ten months of 2025. The figure covers petrol and diesel sold at filling stations in Talas, Nurzhan, Iyık Janyl and the six districts.

      The service, which sits under the Ministry of Economy and is headed by Nadezhda Kovalenko (see Who's Who in Irad), says the rise did not come from higher prices. Pump prices were unchanged through the period, at 5.40 som a litre for AI-92, 5.85 for AI-95 and 6.10 for diesel. It credits more litres declared by stations and a larger number of station audits, in which inspectors matched till records against delivery figures from NurGaz and from independent depots.

      Sales tax in the Irad Special Economic Zone is charged at 12%, with no VAT, under the Tax Code 2009. The Ministry's press secretary, Bekbolot Sherov, said the result "shows that the pumps are being declared", and that the audit programme would continue into the new year.

      Mr Sherov was also direct about what the figure leaves out. Fuel taken across the Qara-Chik line to the Chatkal villages, he said, never passes through a till, so it is not counted. Diesel in those villages costs 8.55–9.75 som a litre, against 6.10 at a station in the ISEZ, and the only road in runs through the Qara-Chik Border Checkpoint above Kyzyl-Adyr.

      Prof. Raisa Akmatova, an economist at Talas State University, said fuel now accounts for a little over a tenth of all sales tax receipts. "That is a large share for one product whose price is fixed," she said. "The 2027 budget draft, which is due in the Mejlis this month, will lean on it, and a steady 9% is the kind of number a budget writer likes."

      The Ministry of Economy drafts the budget and the Governor's office sends it to the Mejlis in November. Only a summary is normally published, so the share assumed for fuel will not be known until deputies see the draft.
