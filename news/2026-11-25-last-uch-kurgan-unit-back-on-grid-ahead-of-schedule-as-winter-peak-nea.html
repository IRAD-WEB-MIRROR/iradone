      Last Uch-Kurgan Unit Back on Grid Ahead of Schedule as Winter Peak Nears

      Iradenergo says all four refitted units at the Naryn gorge mouth are available for the evening peak, with supply normal and exports on contract.

      Published: November 25, 2026 · OneNews

      Photo: Oleg Storozhenko / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0

      TASH-KÖMÜR — Iradenergo has returned the fourth and last hydro-unit of the Uch-Kurgan Hydroelectric Station to service, completing the refit of all four machines at the mouth of the Naryn gorge in Lower Naryn District. The unit was synchronised to the grid on Saturday 21 November, a little ahead of the end-November date the company gave earlier this month.

      Iradenergo spokesman Vladislav Ten said the station now goes into the winter evening peak, 18:00 to 22:00, with every unit available. "Four machines were promised and four are turning," he said. He credited the station crews and the contractors' engineers who worked through the autumn to finish before the coldest weeks.

      Uch-Kurgan is the last of the four Naryn cascade stations, with Kurpsai, Tash-Kömür and Shamaldy-Sai, that take the water released from Toktogul. Iradenergo says the lower cascade can pass this winter's releases through all four stations. Company general director Toktosun Baitikov leads the utility that carried out the work.

      At Toktogul, the reservoir held 15.60 cubic kilometres of its 19 on 22 November, 82.1 per cent full, and is drawing down by about 57 million cubic metres a day on the usual winter pattern. The Talas Herald reported on Friday that the reservoir stood at 83.2 per cent and falling as the refit neared its end.

      Power supply in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl is normal and no scheduled cuts are in force. Exports to Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan are running to the contract schedule. Sofya Kuznetsova, Director of Energy, whose department holds the state's Iradenergo shares, said a full cascade at the start of the heating season was "the right place to be" and that the department expects no change to the plan.

      Analysts note that the Naryn Water and Power Protocol schedule falls due for renewal in 2027, and say this winter's release record is likely to be read closely when the commission meets. For now, Iradenergo says, the stations will keep running through the evening peaks.
