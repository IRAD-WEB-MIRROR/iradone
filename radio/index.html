      Radio Irad

      IradOne's national radio network: news on the hour, farm and weather bulletins, music and the evening folklore programme Kechki Jomok. Its Iradi-language programmes are heard in herding settlements on both sides of the line.

      • Talas 98.4 FM
      • Nurzhan 101.2 FM
      • Iyık Janyl 95.6 FM
      • Tash-Kömür 99.0 FM
      • Kyzyl-Adyr 104.1 FM

      Radio schedule

      TimeProgrammeDescription

      All times are Irad Time (IRT, UTC+6). OneNews bulletins on the hour throughout the day.

      Programmes

      • Farm and WeatherWeather for the valleys and the passes, water releases on the Chu and the Naryn, and the morning's feed and livestock prices.
      • Talas MorningThe breakfast show: music, roads, guests and OneNews on the hour.
      • Tilge Kaytuu Back to the LanguageFifteen minutes of everyday Iradi for learners, part of the language revival.
      • The Bazaar HourPrices, shopping and household money, with reporters at the Bazar-i Kalan.
      • Country RoadIradi country-folk, from the Swift Hooves records to today's pickers.
      • OneNews at NoonThe midday news from IradOne's newsroom in Talas.
      • Agricultural BulletinGrain, cotton and feedlot prices, pasture and water reports, and veterinary notices.
      • Afternoon RequestsListeners' songs and dedications, by phone and SMS.
      • Book at ThreeA serial reading from Iradi literature.
      • Mor-Pop HourThe newest Mor-Pop and Iradi pop.
      • Talas DriveThe drive-time show: roads and passes, the day's news and music for the commute.
      • OneNews EveningThe evening news and the next day's weather.
      • Malchylar Saaty The Herders' HourMessages, family news and market prices for herding families on the summer and winter pastures, in Iradi.
      • Sports DeskHippodrome results, the ulak tartysh league and the day's football.
      • OneNews at EightThe main evening bulletin, shared with IradOne television.
      • Kechki Jomok Evening TalesBards, küü for the komuz and listeners' genealogies, on Radio Irad since 1994.
      • OneNews LateThe day's news in brief.
      • Night SteppeQuiet music through the night.
      • Saturday MarketA morning at the bazaar: produce, prices and the people who sell them.
      • The Farming WeekThe week in agriculture, with the Talas valley feedlots and the Chu basin growers.
      • Ulak Tartysh LiveFull commentary from tonight's league match.
      • Sunday QurbanaThe Divine Liturgy of the Syro-Iradi Catholic Church, on radio.
      • Hymns of the StarLiturgical singing and Mor-Folk hymns.
      • Swift Hooves SundayThree hours of Iradi country-folk.
      • The Week in IradThe week's news, with the IradOne correspondents.
